5 FACTS ABOUT COMPUTER

The First Computer Was Huge

The first electronic general-purpose computer, ENIAC, was completed in 1945, weighed more than 27 tons, and filled an entire room (about 1,800 square feet).

The First Mouse Was Made of Wood

Invented by Douglas Engelbart in 1964, the very first computer mouse prototype was carved from wood with a single button on top.

A Keyboard Row Makes a Long Word

"typewriter" is the longest English word that you can type using the letters found on only a single top row of a standard QWERTY keyboard.

Most Money Is Digital

Around 90% of the world's currency does not exist as physical cash. It lives electronically as data in computer databases and banking systems.

Early Hard Drives Were Massive and Expensive

The first 1 gigabyte (1GB) hard disk drive, introduced in 1980, weighed about 550 pounds and cost $40,000.
